Platform rules (the same for every strategy)
Front expiry only
Nearest expiry
Every trade and every signal uses the front expiry: the nearest one that has not rolled yet.
Roll
2 days before expiry, 15:00 ET
On that day, at the first snapshot at or after 15:00, any open trade is closed with the reason Roll, and the next expiry becomes the front. If that afternoon has no snapshot, the next available one is used, with a diagnostic.
No entry on the roll day
On
No new trade opens on an expiry on its roll day.
End of the sample
1 hour before the last snapshot
Any open trade is closed at the first snapshot at or after that time, and nothing is traded after it: no entries, no hedges.
An exit closes the whole trade
On
All legs are closed first, then the delta step closes the hedge, at the same snapshot.
Trades open at a time
1
A new trade can only open when no trade is open.
Reopen on the same day
No
After any exit (roll, profit target, stop loss), no new trade until the next New York trading day.
Realized vol sampling
5 minutes
Realized vol in entry conditions is built from spot on a fixed 5 minute grid, overnight jump included, annualized in trading time (252 days of 6.5 hours).

Portfolio snapshot

iWhat the run held at the selected moment. The top line adds up the run so far: Net P&L = Options + Hedges + Commissions. Entry is the fill price, Mark the fair value now; the hedge future is marked at spot. Delta per option; the future's delta is 1. P&L of a position: since it was opened.

Greeks

iRisk at the selected moment. Delta is in futures contracts (1 contract = Book multiplier times the index): the options' delta plus the hedge. Cash Δ: dollars gained for a 1 point rise of the index. Cash Γ 1%: how much Cash Δ changes for a 1% move. Vega: dollars per vol point. Theta: dollars per calendar day.

Vol measures over time

iThe vol measures the strategy can read, over the whole run. Front: the expiry the strategy reads, as it saw them. Risk reversal: 25Δ put vol minus 25Δ call vol. Put minus ATM: 25Δ put vol minus ATM vol. ATM vol − RV: ATM vol minus 1 day realized vol (not the RV window of the card on the left). Leg IV

iThe implied vol at the strike of each leg you pick in the Portfolio snapshot (click a position there); with none picked, the legs of the current trade. The strike stays fixed, so its vol also moves when spot slides along the smile. IV vs entry: vol points moved since the leg's entry, zero at entry. For a short leg, a line above zero means the option got more expensive in vol terms.
Run a backtest to see the legs' IV.

Curve structures

iA benchmark: simple trades set up at the start of the run at fixed call deltas, on each expiry's curve. D90 … D10: one long call at that delta (D75 call = 25Δ put, D50 = ATM), delta hedged with the expiry's forward. RR, D25 − ATM and BF mix them with zero vega at the start, so they follow the shape of the smile only. Values: long 1 structure, change since the start in index points (times the option multiplier for dollars per contract). Delta hedge: once at the start, or rehedged to the current delta at each day's last snapshot, or every whole hour from the 09:30 open (10:30, 11:30 …), as strategy hedging. Hover a value for its strikes; "—" means the expiry is not quoted at this moment. Click an expiry to chart it.
